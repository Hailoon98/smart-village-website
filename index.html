Investment & project development model投資及項目開發模式

Five stages from opportunity to decision從機遇到決策的五個階段

01

Origination項目發掘

Identify opportunities through sector research, prospective partners or customer needs.透過行業研究、潛在夥伴或客戶需求發掘機遇。

02

Initial screening初步篩選

Review strategic fit, counterparties, project maturity and information gaps.審視策略契合度、交易對手、項目成熟度及資訊缺口。

03

Due diligence盡職調查

Assess commercial demand, technical feasibility, legal requirements and financial risks.評估商業需求、技術可行性、法律要求及財務風險。

04

Structuring方案架構

Define the partnership model, capital requirements, responsibilities and decision rights.確定合作模式、資金需求、權責分工及決策權。

05

Decision & review決策與監察

Approve or decline, then monitor milestones for any investment that proceeds.批准或否決項目，並持續監察已推進投資的里程碑。

Phase 1 · Focus第一階段 · 聚焦

Define the criteria確立投資標準

Choose priority sectors, define investment criteria and map prospective partners.選定優先領域、訂立投資標準並梳理潛在合作夥伴。

Phase 2 · Validate第二階段 · 驗證

Test feasibility驗證可行性

Assess demand and feasibility, then prepare pilot or partnership proposals with clear decision points.評估需求及可行性，並制定設有明確決策節點的試點或合作方案。

Phase 3 · Develop第三階段 · 發展

Advance what works推進成效項目

Advance approved opportunities through agreements and review results before further expansion.以協議推進獲批項目，並在進一步擴展前檢討成果。
