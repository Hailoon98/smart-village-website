What the plant does工廠業務

The plant assembles, inspects and tests electronic components onto printed circuit boards, following customer-approved designs and bills of materials. It serves industrial customers that need reliable control-board production.工廠按照客戶核准的設計及物料清單，將電子元件組裝、檢測及測試於印刷電路板上，為需要可靠控制板生產的工業客戶提供服務。

It does not make semiconductor chips or bare PCB substrates. It covers the critical stage between component supply and the customer’s finished equipment.工廠並不生產半導體晶片或裸板，而是專注於元件供應與客戶成品設備之間的關鍵製造環節。

Core product: industrial water-pump controller PCBA核心產品：工業水泵控制器電路板組件

The board is the control system inside industrial pump equipment. It reads signals from the pump system and switches control outputs through microcontrollers, power devices, relays, resistors, capacitors and terminal connectors.該控制板是工業水泵設備的控制核心，透過微控制器、功率元件、繼電器、電阻、電容及端子連接器，讀取水泵系統訊號並控制輸出。
